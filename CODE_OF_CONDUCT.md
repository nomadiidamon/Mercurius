# Mercurius Code of Conduct

Mercurius aims to provide a welcoming, respectful space for everyone who
participates in the project, including through issues, pull requests, code
reviews, and other project discussions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when disagreeing.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Focus discussion on the work and its impact; do not make personal attacks.
- Respect people's privacy and differing experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, and sharing another person's private information without consent
are not acceptable. This applies in project spaces and in public spaces when
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior in a Mercurius project
space, contact the project maintainers privately through GitHub. Do not use a
public issue to report a sensitive personal matter.

Project maintainers will review reports, take appropriate action, and handle
reports as confidentially as they can. They may remove or edit contributions
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Maintainers are
expected to follow these standards themselves and to apply them consistently.
